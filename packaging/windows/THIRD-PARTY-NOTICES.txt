oriel bundles these libraries as separate DLLs:

FFmpeg (avcodec, avutil, swresample, swscale)
  LGPL 2.1 or later; the build from https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds
  (the "lgpl-shared" variant). Its license is in FFmpeg-LICENSE.txt; source
  is available from https://ffmpeg.org and the build recipes from the link
  above. The DLLs can be replaced with compatible builds.

PyroWave (libpyrowave-shared-1.dll)
  MIT license; https://github.com/Themaister/pyrowave, with Granite
  (https://github.com/Themaister/Granite, MIT) and volk
  (https://github.com/zeux/volk, MIT).

MinGW-w64 winpthreads (libwinpthread-1.dll)
  MIT and BSD-style licenses; https://www.mingw-w64.org.

The service uses the ViGEmBus driver for virtual gamepads when installed
separately (https://github.com/nefarius/ViGEmBus) and the SudoVDA driver
for extra displays when installed separately
(https://github.com/SudoMaker/SudoVDA); neither is included.
