in THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES-RUST.txt. It bundles these libraries as separate
DLLs:

FFmpeg (avcodec, avutil, swresample, swscale)
  LGPL 2.1 or later; the build from https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds
  (release autobuild-2026-10-21-13-06, the "lgpl-shared" variant of
  n8.1.3-20-gc8de997cb1). Its license is in FFmpeg-LICENSE.txt. Its source
  is FFmpeg commit c8de997cb1 (https://git.ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg.git), built
  with the scripts in that BtbN release. The DLLs can be replaced with
  compatible builds.

PyroWave (libpyrowave-shared-1.dll), with parts of Granite, volk and
Vulkan-Headers compiled in, and MinGW-w64 winpthreads (libwinpthread-1.dll)
  Built by Nix; listed with their licenses at the end of this file, the
  license texts in the directories named there.

The service uses the ViGEmBus driver for virtual gamepads when installed
separately (https://github.com/nefarius/ViGEmBus) and the SudoVDA driver
for extra displays when installed separately
(https://github.com/SudoMaker/SudoVDA); neither is included.
